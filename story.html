The beginning

In 2000, Mr. Nworie Chukwuma Kingsley opened the doors of Chuks Collections in Wuse, Abuja — with a simple vision: to give men a place to dress with distinction.

What began as one boutique built a reputation on something rare — shoes made and chosen with real craftsmanship, the kind that changes how a man carries himself.

That reputation grew. In 2014, Chuks Collections expanded to Kubwa, Abuja, bringing the same standard of quality to a new generation of clients across the city.

Today, with two locations and over two decades of experience, Chuks Collections remains true to where it started — a boutique for the man who knows that the right pair of shoes isn’t just footwear. It’s a foundation.
